What is Pearl?

Pearl is a first-of-its-kind blockchain built on **Useful Proof of Work**. Like other blockchains, Pearl is a decentralized network that records transactions securely and transparently without relying on a central authority.

What makes Pearl different is _how_ the network is secured. Instead of wasting computing power on meaningless calculations, Pearl uses GPU computation that performs **real, economically useful work**, such as AI inference. This means the same GPU effort both secures the blockchain _and_ produces valuable AI services.

What is "Useful Proof of Work"?

Traditional Proof of Work (PoW), used by early blockchains, secures the network by having computers solve cryptographic puzzles. While effective, these computations have no use outside of blockchain security.

Pearl introduces **Useful Proof of Work**, where mining is performed as a _by-product_ of real AI computation. Using Pearl's **2-for-1 Proof of Useful Work model**, GPUs execute AI workloads based on matrix multiplication (MatMul / GEMM), which simultaneously:

- Secure the Pearl blockchain
- Deliver real AI inference services

This converts GPU computation anywhere in the world into **fungible monetary value**, with negligible overhead and minimal additional energy usage.
