Author: TIGER

Recently, the crypto community has been buzzing about @prlnet's Pearl mining. Essentially, it’s about renting a 4090/5090 GPU on a cloud computing platform and mining coins. As Toni put it, the daily profit from mining and selling is over 50%. With the current coin price at 1.3–1.4U, as long as you can rent a fully powered 4090 for under 0.7U, you’re in the green.

This piece breaks down the process to a foolproof level, and at the end, it includes a profit model that you can calculate yourself. Don’t just rely on the hype; make sure you do the math before jumping in.

**1\. What is Pearl? (Understand in 30 seconds)**

- **PoUW (Proof of Useful Work) L1 public chain: Miners' GPUs are not just calculating meaningless hashes; they are performing matrix multiplications in AI inference/training. Mining and real computational power are essentially the same, with additional overhead of only about 10%.**

- **Mainnet launch in April 2026, already partnered with Together AI (NVIDIA cloud partner) to subsidize AI computing costs through token emissions (Gemma-4-31B-it-pearl endpoint directly offers a 25% discount).**

- **No pre-mining, no founder shares, no VC, fair launch — this is the strongest point of its narrative.**


In short: the higher the crypto price, the cheaper AI reasoning becomes, and the more people will mine → this is a self-reinforcing flywheel.

**Two, preparations before mining**

You only need two things:

- An account that can rent 4090/5090 cloud computing power

- A Pearl wallet address


Card rental platforms (choose one, beginners recommended to use RunPod for user-friendly interface):

- **RunPod 👉 https://runpod.io?ref=4rltlnx9**

- **VastAI 👉 https://cloud.vast.ai/?ref\_id=559971**


**Steps three and four to start mining (beginner-friendly)**

**Step 1 \| Rent a card on RunPod/VastAI, filter for RTX 4090 / 5090, make sure to choose 'full power cards' (complete cards, not fractional slices). Card rental rule: rent at 0.7 U/hour (based on current crypto prices, this is the break-even point, which I'll calculate for you in section four).**

**Step 2 \| Generate a Pearl wallet Open 👉 http://compute.pearlresearch.ai/wallet to generate it. Be sure to write down the mnemonic offline, and copy your wallet address for backup.**

**Step 3 \| Configure the miner Open 👉 https://pearlhash.xyz/#config, input the wallet address from the previous step, the page will generate a one-click start command/script, copy it. (pearlhash pool fee is 3%, minimum withdrawal of 1 PRL, very low threshold.)**

**Step 4 \| SSH into the instance to get it running**

- Set up SSH keys for the instance in RunPod/VastAI and connect.

- Paste and execute the command copied in step 3.

- Once you see the computing power reporting and the coins start flowing → you can clock out, just let it run.


The miner is essentially the official vLLM miner (CUDA). Once running, the GPU will produce PRL while providing AI reasoning power externally.

Withdraw and sell: After accumulating enough PRL, you can cash out via OTC (otc.lordofpearls.xyz / pearl-otc.com/marketplace) or the cross-chain bridge pearlbridge.xyz.

**Four, cost / revenue calculation (key point, calculate your own accounts, don’t get carried away)**

Don't just trust 'daily returns of 50%+', calculate your own formulas:

**Core formula**

Daily net profit = Daily PRL produced per card × crypto price × (1 − 3% pool fee) − hourly rental fee × 24

**The simplest judgment method (recommended): as long as it meets: the PRL value produced per hour per card > hourly rental fee, you’re making money. This is exactly where Toni's saying comes from: 'Renting below 0.7 U means profit' — currently, a 4090 produces a value of about 0.7 U per hour.**

**Break-even point (example, please fill in real-time data from the pearlhash panel)**

- Assuming rental fee = 0.6 U/hour → daily cost = 0.6 × 24 = 14.4 U

- Assuming crypto price = 1.3 U

- Then you need to produce at least: 14.4 ÷ (1.3 × 0.97) ≈ 11.4 PRL/day to break even.

- Anything above this amount is pure profit.


**Two times leverage**

- **The lower the rental fee, the better: keep an eye on spot/bid instances, 0.4–0.5 U for the 4090 has the largest profit margin.**

- **Crypto prices are a double-edged sword: profits double when prices rise, but drop below the rental line and you're in the red — that's why you need to check your accounts daily, don’t just set and forget.**


**Five, risk warning (must read)**

- **Crypto price volatility: All calculations are based on 1.3 U. If the price halves, you'll go from profit to loss, and early tokens can be extremely volatile.**

- **Sell slippage: PRL currently relies mainly on OTC/bridges for liquidity, with limited depth. Large sell orders will face slippage and delayed transaction times.**

- **Renting cards burns money by the second: if the instance isn't fully utilized, if there's a config error, or if the network drops, you won't mine but the rental fees will still be charged.**

- **Early project uncertainty: The mainnet just launched, partnerships and narratives are appealing, but this also means rules, outputs, and pools can be adjusted at any time.**


**Conclusion: This isn't a guaranteed profit scheme; it's about 'making money while the arbitrage window exists for those who can calculate'. Rent one card to run for 24 hours, gather real data, plug it into the formula above to verify profitability, then decide whether to leverage up.**
