# The Pearl Protocol

Pearl’s mathematical breakthrough enables every GPU cycle powering AI systems to simultaneously produce a native digital currency.

[Download the Whitepaper](https://pearlresearch.ai/Pearl_Whitepaper.pdf) [Join the Network](https://github.com/pearl-research-labs/pearl)

- [**Proofs of Useful Work From Arbitrary Matrix Multiplication** \\
\\
Revisits the longstanding open problem of Nakamoto consensus on real computation the miner chooses, and gives a proof of useful work for matrix multiplication with 1 + o(1) overhead, so GPUs can reuse their AI compute to secure a chain.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09971)
- [**The Economics of Proof-of-Useful-Work** \\
\\
A competitive-equilibrium model of a PoUW blockchain: closed-form prices across mining, inference, and "duplex" work, plus why a majority attack stays tied to the block reward once prices adjust.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06700)
- [**Problems We Are Working On** \\
\\
The open questions in mathematics, cryptography, and consensus that Pearl is still working to solve.](https://pearlpolymath.com/)
- [**Pearl INT Whitepaper** \\
\\
The original whitepaper: the Pearl protocol with an exact integer (INT) matrix-multiplication mining kernel.](https://pearlresearch.ai/research/int-whitepaper)
